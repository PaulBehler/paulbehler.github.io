Welcome! I am a PhD Student in Economics at the University of Bonn and the Cluster of Excellence ECONtribute. 

My papers touch several fields of applied microeconomics, such as cultural economics, environmental economics, the economics of education and political economy. Currently, I am particularly interested in studying the determinants of cultural values and norms. Feel free to contact me at [paul.behler@uni-bonn.de](mailto:paul.behler@uni-bonn.de).
