excerpt: "Cultural transmission may perpetuate inequality if advantaged parents intentionally shape their children's preferences toward traits that facilitate economic success. We survey 1,200 U.S. parents about their preferences for transmitting seven cultural traits. For most traits, parents across the education spectrum align: they want children to be more patient, altruistic, and hard-working. Risk attitude is the exception. Preferences for risk-seeking rise monotonically with education—non-college parents want slightly less risk-taking for their children; master's-educated parents strongly want more—with no other trait showing a comparable gradient. Since risk-taking predicts entrepreneurship and income, this gradient may contribute to persistent inequality."
